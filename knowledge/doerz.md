# Doerz — agency projects (Junaid as founder & CTO)

Junaid founded and was CTO of **Doerz** (doerz.tech), a full-stack software development agency building products from scratch for medium-to-large startups and businesses worldwide. He scaled the engineering org from 2 to 30 engineers and delivered 40+ production platforms across many sectors. Below is a representative set of client projects (research, UI/UX design, mobile + web app development). Most were delivered for international clients (USA, UK, Canada, Europe). For deeper detail, point people to doerz.tech/case-studies.

## MindBody Climb — rock-climbing coaching app (Fitness)
A rock-climbing coaching app for intermediate climbers: structured training system, mental exercises, tutorials & events, tracking & analytics, real-time feedback, subscription model. Research, UI/UX, mobile + web app. Client: Zach Fletcher (USA), 2023–2024.

## Natyfit — connecting gym-goers (Fitness)
Connects users to find workout partners, share physical activities, and chat live. Features: workout-partner matching, fitness event calendar, personalised plans, achievement badges, activity sharing. Research, UI/UX, mobile app. Client: Micah Cunningham (USA), 2022.

## Fit Flow — fitness companion (Fitness)
All-in-one fitness companion: workout tracking & progress monitoring, wearable integration, meal planning, personalised plans, community forums, calorie counter. Research, UI/UX, mobile + web app. Client: Zach Fletcher (USA), 2023–2024.

## Schedy / GYM-BOOK — gym management (Fitness / Management)
Combines a gym-management system with a fitness-industry social platform: business/member/staff management, reporting & analytics, billing & invoicing. Research, UI/UX, mobile + web app. Client: Sokol Petro (Italy), 2022.

## OffSzn — athletes/coaches/sponsors network (Fitness / Sports)
Social network connecting athletes, athletic programs, and collegiate coaches with sponsors and businesses: sports event calendar, sponsorship opportunities, achievement sharing, team collaboration. Research, UI/UX, mobile + web app. Client: Dorian Flowers (USA), 2021.

## Gym Community — fitness social platform (Fitness)
Community for gym-goers via group discussions, achievement sharing, and messaging: workout-partner finding, event calendar, routine sharing, live chat. Research, UI/UX, mobile app. Client: Matt Fisher (USA), 2022–2023.

## Achēv — non-profit employment agency app (Non-Profit)
App for Achēv, one of the largest providers of employment, settlement, language, women, youth and technology services across the GTA, Canada: application process, events & workshops, news & articles, app-wide search, location-based filters, personalised feed. Mobile & web. Client: Charles Curl / Achēv (Canada), 2024.

## BookCyp — appointment scheduling (Bookings / Health & Beauty)
Appointment scheduling & management for health-and-beauty businesses: booking & management, service-provider profiles, in-app messaging, reviews & ratings, payment integration. Research, UI/UX, mobile + web app. Client: Stylios (Cyprus), 2023.

## Fertility Connects — healthcare community (Healthcare)
Community-centric platform for navigating the fertility journey: profile management, chat & forums, full admin CMS, reports & suspensions, feedback & analytics, information hub. Research, UI/UX, web + mobile. Client: Racheal, 2024.

## Patient & Doctor Connection (Healthcare)
Platform connecting health workers and patients: video calling, online appointment booking, doctor search, health records, prescription requests, payments, patient profiles, forums. Research, UI/UX, web + mobile. Client: Muhammad Naeem, 2020.

## CircleGeo — family safety & location tracking (Safety)
Family-safety and location-tracking app: real-time location tracking, geofencing, Bluetooth car detection, in-app family chat, live driving tracking, emergency SOS. Research, UI/UX, mobile app. Client: Gabriel Obassi (UK), 2023–2024.

## MvM (Man v Media) — influencer social network (Online Communities)
Connects media influencers with like-minded people: find influencers by interest, recommendations, trending topics/challenges, interest-based feeds, live chat. Research, UI/UX, mobile app. Client: Spenser Benson (USA), 2022.

## Whinny — horse-riding community (Online Communities)
Connects the horse community and manages horse-care tasks: messaging with owners, horse health records, community forums, care task management, event calendar. Research, UI/UX, mobile + web app. Client: Raul Blanco (Sweden), 2023.

## Fitty — clothing design feedback (E-commerce)
E-commerce app for clothing designers to get consumer feedback before manufacturing: design preview/prototyping, pre-order & crowdfunding, designer profiles, sales analytics, feedback/commenting. Research, UI/UX, mobile + web app. Client: Pierce Maloney (USA), 2021.

## Food Truckin — food-truck discovery (E-commerce / Bookings)
