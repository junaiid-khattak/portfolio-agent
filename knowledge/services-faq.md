# Working with Junaid — services, process, FAQ

## What he builds
Production AI products, end to end: realtime voice agents (OpenAI Realtime API + WebRTC), autonomous / agentic workflows (orchestrator + specialist sub-agents with guardrails, versioned action ledgers, kill-switches), RAG systems (pgvector, retrieval tuning, evals), and full-stack AI SaaS MVPs — frontend, backend, infra, billing, and the dashboard customers actually use. Strongest areas: realtime voice AI and agentic systems.

## Who he helps
1. Individuals/founders with an idea who need it architected and shipped fast.
2. Businesses wondering where AI actually fits — he finds the one or two highest-leverage places and builds them.
3. Companies hiring agentic/AI talent — he architects and ships production agent systems end to end.

## How he works (the system behind the speed)
A real, tracked AI-native workflow that does the work a whole team used to: Claude & ChatGPT for planning/research → Notion as the brain and database → Claude Code & Codex as executors with skills tuned per platform → Stitch for UI/UX → a fleet of sub-agents that pick up assigned tasks, execute them, and update their status, so nothing slips and the whole pipeline is visible. The discipline of a senior team, run by one person. This is why builds that traditionally take a team many months ship solo in weeks.

## The agency-vs-AI thesis (his honest framing)
He ran a software agency, so he knows where the months go: discovery, specs, coordination across roles, handoffs, QA cycles, review loops — real, expensive structural overhead. AI made most of that overhead optional. So he passes the speed and cost advantage to the client instead of an agency owner pocketing it. He's not knocking agencies (he built one) — software development just fundamentally changed, and he rebuilt around it.

## Availability & engagement
He keeps his calendar deliberately small: up to 10 consultations a week (about two a day) and never more than 3 builds running at once, which keeps each project on a weeks-not-months timeline. Open to: fixed-scope MVP/feature builds, fractional lead/CTO engagements, and senior hourly work; also full-time remote roles for the right team. Comp is flexible / discussed on a call — he doesn't publish fixed rates because scope varies a lot.

## How to start
The lowest-friction first step is a free intro call (the "AI Build Call", ~20 min) — talk through what you're building and he'll tell you honestly how he'd build it, what it'd take, and whether you even need him. Book it from the site, or the digital twin can grab a real slot. He'll come prepared with concrete ideas.
